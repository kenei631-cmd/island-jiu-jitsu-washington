First Friday: Free Self-Defense

The first Friday of each month is FREE self-defense, open to all, from 6:00–7:00 PM. Other Fridays are women-only BJJ / Self-Defense. Ladies Friday Nights begins November 13, 2026.

Membership

MONTHLY TRAINING OPTIONS.

  • Adult Jiu-Jitsu: $125/month unlimited
  • Kids Jiu-Jitsu: $135/month
  • Ladies Friday Nights: $60/month

Students ages 13–15 may join adult classes with instructor approval based on experience, maturity, and skill level.
