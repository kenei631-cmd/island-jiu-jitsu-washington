START WITH THE BASICS.

Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington serves students from Arlington, Smokey Point, and nearby communities at one local academy.

Where is Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington located?

Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington is located at 3131 Smokey Point Dr, Ste 11B, Arlington, WA 98223, in the Smokey Point area of Arlington. Explore Jiu-Jitsu near Smokey Point. Explore Jiu-Jitsu near Lakewood and North Marysville.

What Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu programs do you offer in Arlington?

Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington offers adult Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, kids Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu, and Ladies Friday Nights, a women-only class.

What ages can train at Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington?

Kids Jiu-Jitsu is for ages 6–16. Adult Jiu-Jitsu is for ages 16 and up. Students ages 13–15 may join the adult class with instructor approval based on experience, maturity, and skill level.

When are Jiu-Jitsu classes held?

Adult Jiu-Jitsu has No-Gi classes Monday and Tuesday from 9:30–11:00 AM with Coach Micah, and a Gi class Thursday from 9:30–11:00 AM with Coach Brandon. Evening adult classes are Monday Gi, Tuesday No-Gi, and Thursday Gi from 7:00–8:00 PM. Adult Open Mat / long sparring is Wednesday from 5:00–7:00 PM. Youth Jiu-Jitsu is Monday Gi, Tuesday No-Gi, and Thursday Gi from 5:15–6:45 PM. Ladies Friday Nights runs Fridays from 6:00–7:00 PM, and Saturday Open Mat / long sparring runs from 10:00 AM–Noon. Check the current schedule before registering because class times can change.

Do you offer a women-only Jiu-Jitsu class?

Yes. Ladies Friday Nights is a women-only class on Fridays from 6:00–7:00 PM with Coach Lauren and Coach Leilani. The program is scheduled to start November 13, 2026.
