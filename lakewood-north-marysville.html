Where can I train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu near Lakewood and North Marysville, WA?

Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington is located at 3131 Smokey Point Dr, Ste 11B, Arlington, WA 98223. The academy serves adults, kids, and women from Lakewood, North Marysville, Smokey Point, and Arlington.

Is this the Lakewood area near North Marysville?

Yes. This page is for the Lakewood and North Marysville area in Snohomish County, near Arlington and Smokey Point. Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington trains from its Arlington academy on Smokey Point Drive.

Which Jiu-Jitsu programs are available for Lakewood and North Marysville students?

Students can choose Adult Jiu-Jitsu, Kids Jiu-Jitsu for ages 6–16, and Ladies Friday Nights, a women-only class. See the current class schedule and pricing before registering.

Can beginners start Jiu-Jitsu near Lakewood and North Marysville?

Yes. Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington welcomes people who are new to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. The academy focuses on technical instruction, challenging training, and a respectful team environment.
