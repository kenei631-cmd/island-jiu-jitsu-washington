Smokey Point Jiu-Jitsu questions

HELPFUL ANSWERS BEFORE YOU START.

For more detailed answers about ages, class times, and getting started, visit the Arlington Jiu-Jitsu FAQ.

Where can I train Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu near Smokey Point, WA?

Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington is located at 3131 Smokey Point Dr, Ste 11B, Arlington, WA 98223. The academy serves the Smokey Point area with adult, kids, and women-only Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu programs.

Which Jiu-Jitsu classes are available for Smokey Point students?

Smokey Point students can choose Adult Jiu-Jitsu, Kids Jiu-Jitsu for ages 6–16, and Ladies Friday Nights, a women-only class. See the current class schedule and pricing before registering.

Can beginners start Jiu-Jitsu near Smokey Point?

Yes. Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington welcomes students who are new to Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu. The academy focuses on technical instruction, challenging training, and a team environment that supports progress.

How do I get started with a Smokey Point Jiu-Jitsu class?

Review the current schedule and programs, then contact Island Jiu-Jitsu Washington to ask about the next step or register online.
